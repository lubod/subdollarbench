## Why

The `.50` bar (`sdb-gates/flake-13231b457717.txt`, 2026-11-18) takes
about 8 hours: one pass of its 30 proofs is about 24 minutes. Per proof
(seconds per run, from that record): the two stage-20 proofs 221 and
220 (each about 15 % of the bar, and **both** grade the whole ladder
0-20), `r45_f1_reference_scores_one` 364 (5 runs), the ticket-2-less
reference 79, the rest 1-68 (most about 26).

And the bar is not catching what costs money. It grades known-good
reference servers 20 times, so it measures **flakiness**, not
**correctness**: the coverage reading (round 88), the proxy buffering
(round 87), the torn tail and the ticket-2 syntax (round 89) all passed
every bar. Operator, 2026-11-18: "is there any possibility to make this
bar optimized and faster and effective?", then "91" (all four items,
including fewer passes for untouched proofs: a change to DECISIONS #26,
recorded as #37).

### B4. Proofs that catch a wrong grade, not just a flaky one

    torn tail;
  - stage 11: a store with no index (a full scan) fails the perf gate;
  - stage 6 or 12: a store that lets a dirty read or a lost update
    through;
  - stage 15: a test suite that stays green when the feature is reverted.

  workspaces from past runs (at least two: one haiku `.48` phase-4

  or keys), each with its expected verdict per stage. A proof regrades
  them and fails when a verdict changes; a round that changes a verdict
  on purpose updates the expectation and says why in its notes.
- These run 3 times per bar (they are deterministic fixtures; their job
  is to catch a grader change, not flakiness).
