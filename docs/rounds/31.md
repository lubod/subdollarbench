    `src/pipeline.rs:6366`).

    (`scripts/flake-bar.sh:115`).

  The `infra_error` check (27 `assert!(!out.infra_error` sites) and the
  final `(passed_stages, total_stages)` check do not.

from zero. Fixing the `infra_error` itself waits for that evidence.
