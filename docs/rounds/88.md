## Why

The reviewer's analysis of the `.48` haiku grid
(`runs/.grid-v48b-haiku-ledger.tsv`, 2026-11-17):

1. **Coverage measured on stale build output.** In phase 4, many
   attempts failed with `broke ticket 15 ... [tests] coverage measurement
   failed (cargo test or llvm-cov): ... no source lines under src/`
   while every candidate test passed. The measurement
   (`measure_rust_coverage`, `src/pipeline.rs:496-564`) builds into the
   candidate's own `target/` and trusts what is there. The reviewer
   reproduced it on career 0's last phase-4 workspace
   (`campaign_sdb-sql_openrouter_anthropic_claude-haiku-5.5_20261116_204907_w1/workspace`),
   in `subdollar-sandbox:latest`, with the script from
   `src/pipeline.rs:500-545`:
   - with the workspace's `target/` in place: exit 3, `no source lines
     under src/` (only `tests/pg.rs` in the report);
   - the same tree with `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=/tmp/sdb-cov-target`, or
     without `target/`: `coverage: 85.62%`.
   It was the final failure on up to ~25 points of haiku's phase 4
   (career 0 stages 16 and 20; career 1 stage 18 and the full attempts at
   19 and 20) and appears in 5 of the void `.47` runs. A planted
   `target/` could also steer the reading.
2. **The bill misses earlier attempts.** A stage with two rounds records
   only the last round's bill: career 0 stage 16 shows `api_cost_usd`
   $0.142 and `billed_usd` $0.012. The phases' `billed_usd` add up to
   $7.02; the key's spend over the grid (the `[KEY] initial account-key
   spend` reading at the first phase, $111.4773, and the key's usage
   after the last, $124.6627) is $13.19, and nothing else paid ran in
   between. `BillingProxy::start` removes `billed.jsonl` at every start
   (`src/sandbox/opencode.rs:798-799`); where the per-stage figure loses
   the earlier rounds is for the implementer to find (`src/pipeline.rs:3168`,
   `:3190`, `src/campaign.rs:3789`).

## Items

### F1. Coverage is measured on a fresh build

`measure_rust_coverage` builds and runs the tests with
`CARGO_TARGET_DIR` set to an empty directory outside `/workspace`
(removed before each measurement), so nothing the candidate leaves in
`target/` is read or reused. The rest of the measurement is unchanged
(the same `cargo test` commands, the `/workspace/src/` filter, the
80 % gate). If the fresh build needs more than the 300 s limit
(`src/pipeline.rs:548`), say so under **Spec questions** with the
measured time; don't change the limit silently.

Acceptance:
- [ ] A workspace whose `target/` holds stale instrumented output (the
      reviewer's reproduction, or a fixture that builds, edits a source
      file with an older mtime, and measures) measures the true
      coverage, not `no source lines under src/` — test:
      `r88_f1_coverage_ignores_a_stale_target`
- [ ] A planted `target/` (prebuilt test binaries or profraw files)
      can't change the reading — test:
      `r88_f1_planted_target_is_ignored`
- [ ] Each seen failing against today's measurement

### F2. A stage's bill counts every attempt and round

Every agent session's calls reach the stage's and the phase's
`billed_usd`: full, lite and catch-up attempts, repair rounds
(`rounds[1]`), resumes, and sessions that end in an error. The sum of a
phase's `billed.jsonl` records equals the phase's `billed_usd`. Keep
each session's records (append, or one file per session) instead of
deleting them at the next start.

Acceptance:
- [ ] A stage with two rounds, each with stub calls at known costs:
      `billed_usd` is the sum of both rounds — test:
      `r88_f2_billed_counts_every_round`
- [ ] A phase's `billed_usd` equals the sum of every record the proxy
      wrote during the phase (stub upstream) — test:
      `r88_f2_phase_bill_equals_the_proxy_log`
- [ ] Each seen failing
