## Why

- **The reference port** (round 74, R74-1): every published phase of
  `2026-10-19.45` was told `ref-sql`'s port as its lane's port (5580,
  5680, 5780) instead of 5480; round 74 fixed the renderer
  (`template_ports_for_worker`, `src/config.rs`). The prompt the
  candidate sees changed, which needs a rules bump (AGENTS.md, "Rules
  version"). Until this round merges, no paid cell runs on master.
- **The sandbox's omp** (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #28): `Dockerfile.sandbox`
  pins `OMP_VERSION=18.4.2`, the local image runs 18.8.0, CI builds
  18.4.2 fresh, and 18.8.0 resolves `tencent/hy4` to `hy4-preview`. A
  bump is the moment to move to a current omp, pinned for real.
- **What goes on the new board:** the 6 ranked models of `2026-10-19.45`,
  minimax (withdrawn, `docs/DECISIONS.md` #29), and the new ones
  (glm-5.3-flash, ling-3.1-flash, hy4). Round 73's pins fold in here;
  73 is superseded.
