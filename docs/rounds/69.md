## Why

The pages are correct and readable after rounds 64-66. They still look
like an engineering console, not a public benchmark page. The reviewer's
read of `https://www.subdollarbench.org/` (2026-11-06 to 11-08):

- **The header speaks harness:** "cap policy: at list price
  (model_prices.json) for runs from .39; each career records its own."
  A reader learns nothing from it.
- **The four cards** (Careers 15, Models 5, Best points 70.0, Spend
  $35.46) don't answer what a visitor asks: who wins, what it costs,
  what's cheapest per point.
- **The table:**
  - 12 columns of equal weight; points, the headline number, look like
    every other number;
  - the ticket ladder and agreement strips are hidden behind
    `×3 careers ▸`;
  - the dot strips are small and grey.
- **Colours:** each chart picks its own; one model has a different
  colour on the board, on "Points against dollars" and on "Survival".
- **The results page** is one long scroll of tables and charts, with no
  way to jump to a section, and charts without axis titles.
- **Nothing to share:** no favicon, description, footer, or link to the
  code and method.

No graded path: `src/report/grid.rs`, `src/report/portal.rs`,
`src/report/portal_board.css`. Fast gate, and no bar under any rule.

## Constraints (all items)

- **Self-contained static HTML**, as today: no web fonts, CDN, external
  JS or images. The pages must work from a zip on any host.
  `<details>`/`<summary>` are the only interactivity, and a small inline
  `<script>` is allowed for nothing essential.
- **Size:** each page at most 150 KB with 15 careers (index is about
  100 KB today).
- **The dark theme stays.** Text contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body
  text, 3:1 for large text and chart marks against their background).
- **The phone layout** (rounds 65 and 66: 390 px, no sideways scroll for
  rank, name, points, perf and spend) keeps working.
- **Every number on the page today stays reachable** (on the row, in
  details, or in a title). This round removes clutter, not data.
