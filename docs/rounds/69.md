# Round 69: a facelift for the public pages (board and results)

Status: draft (operator, 2026-11-08: "prepare ... facelift of portal, try to make it nicer")
Opened: <date>, base `round-69-open`
Branch: `round69`
Rules: `2026-10-19.45` (unchanged: display only)

## Why

The pages are correct and readable after rounds 64-66. They still look
like an engineering console, not a public benchmark page. The reviewer's
read of `https://www.subdollarbench.org/` (2026-11-06 to 11-08):

- **The header speaks harness:** "cap policy: at list price
  (model_prices.json) for runs from .39; each career records its own."
  A reader learns nothing from it.
- **The four cards** (Careers 15, Models 5, Best points 70.0, Spend
  $35.46) don't answer what a visitor asks: who wins, what it costs,
  what's cheapest per point.
- **The table:**
  - 12 columns of equal weight; points, the headline number, look like
    every other number;
  - the ticket ladder and agreement strips are hidden behind
    `×3 careers ▸`;
  - the dot strips are small and grey.
- **Colours:** each chart picks its own; one model has a different
  colour on the board, on "Points against dollars" and on "Survival".
- **The results page** is one long scroll of tables and charts, with no
  way to jump to a section, and charts without axis titles.
- **Nothing to share:** no favicon, description, footer, or link to the
  code and method.

No graded path: `src/report/grid.rs`, `src/report/portal.rs`,
`src/report/portal_board.css`. Fast gate, and no bar under any rule.

## Constraints (all items)

- **Self-contained static HTML**, as today: no web fonts, CDN, external
  JS or images. The pages must work from a zip on any host.
  `<details>`/`<summary>` are the only interactivity, and a small inline
  `<script>` is allowed for nothing essential.
- **Size:** each page at most 150 KB with 15 careers (index is about
  100 KB today).
- **The dark theme stays.** Text contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body
  text, 3:1 for large text and chart marks against their background).
- **The phone layout** (rounds 65 and 66: 390 px, no sideways scroll for
  rank, name, points, perf and spend) keeps working.
- **Every number on the page today stays reachable** (on the row, in
  details, or in a title). This round removes clutter, not data.

## Items

### F1. One visual system for both pages

- **A palette with one colour per model,** assigned in board order, and
  stable across the board, every chart, every legend and the results
  tables (a swatch beside the name). Eight colours that stay distinct on
  the dark background and for common colour-vision deficiencies (for
  example the Okabe-Ito set).
- **Type scale and spacing tokens** in CSS custom properties (`--fs-*`,
  `--sp-*`). Headings, numbers and labels use them consistently.
  Numbers use tabular figures.
- **Header:**
  - the title;
  - one plain sentence: "Budget LLMs build a SQL database, ticket by
    ticket, for at most $0.99 per phase. Graded against SQLite.";
  - the updated time.

  The rules, fingerprint, cap basis and price list move to the results
  page's "What it measured".
- **Footer** on both pages: a GitHub link, the methodology
  (`docs/PROPOSAL-sdb-sql.md`), the rules version and "Updated ...".
- **`<meta name="description">`**, `<meta property="og:title">` and
  `og:description`, and an inline SVG favicon (`data:` URL).

Acceptance:
- [ ] One model has the same colour on the board row, both charts'
  legends and the results tables. Test: `r69_f1_one_colour_per_model`
  (it parses the export).
- [ ] The board's visible text no longer contains `model_prices.json` or
  `cap policy`; the results page does. Test: `r69_f1_header_plain`.
- [ ] Both pages have the footer, the meta description and the favicon.

### F2. The board leads with the answer

- **Summary cards** that answer questions:
  - **Top model** (name, median points);
  - **Best points per dollar** (name, value);
  - **Cheapest career** (name, $);
  - **Careers** (N across M models).
- **Model rows:**
  - **Points are the hero:** a large median with `/ 76`, and under it a
    0-76 bar with every career as a dot, the median marked, and the
    best-worst range shaded. This replaces the small grey dot strip for
    points.
  - **Perf** and **$ per career** as compact badges: perf coloured
    above or below 1.0×.
  - **The ticket-agreement strip** (21 squares) shows on the row, not
    only in details.
  - Secondary numbers (pts/$, duration, turns, tokens, highest ticket,
    kept green) go in one muted line under the name, or a "details"
    column. Their dot strips stay in the expanded view.
- **Rank:** `#1` large, with the share (`70%`) beside it as a small badge
  and the full distribution in its title, as today.
- **The expanded careers** keep their current content, restyled to match.

Acceptance:
- [ ] The four cards show the right values on the 15-career fixture or
  the real export (deepseek top at 65.0; the best pts/$ and the cheapest
  career computed from the data). Test: `r69_f2_summary_cards`.
- [ ] Each model row contains its agreement strip outside `<details>`.
  Test: `r69_f2_agreement_on_row`.
- [ ] 1440 px and 390 px screenshots of the real board in the notes, with

  (`~/.cache/ms-playwright/chromium_headless_shell-1208/chrome-headless-shell-linux64/chrome-headless-shell --no-sandbox --headless --screenshot=... --window-size=1440,1400 file:///tmp/x/index.html`).

### F3. The results page reads as a report

- **A sticky section nav** at the top (What it measured · Points vs $ ·
  Survival · Per-ticket · Head-to-head · Variance · Ranks), with anchor
  links. On a phone, a horizontal scroll row.
- **Charts:**
  - axis titles ("$ per career", "points (of 76)", "ticket");
  - gridlines at round values;
  - the model palette from F1;
  - at least 560 px tall on desktop, scaling down on a phone;
  - legends above each chart.
- **Tables:** zebra rows, right-aligned numbers, sticky header rows on
  long tables, and the model swatch beside every model name.
- "What it measured" becomes a two-column card: rules, fingerprints,
  image, harness, cap, prices, grid dates.

Acceptance:
- [ ] The nav has a working anchor for each section (each target id
  exists). Test: `r69_f3_section_nav`.
- [ ] Both charts carry axis titles. Test: `r69_f3_axis_titles`.
- [ ] 1440 px and 390 px screenshots of the real results page in the
  notes.

### F4. The quality bar

- **Contrast:** a test computes the contrast of the CSS palette's text
  and chart colours against their backgrounds, and fails below AA. Test:
  `r69_f4_contrast`.
- **Size:** a test exports the 15-career fixture and asserts each page is
  at most 150 KB. Test: `r69_f4_page_size`.
- **Self-contained:** a test asserts no `http(s)://` in any `src=`,
  `<link href=`, or CSS `url(` (`<a href>` links are fine). Test:
  `r69_f4_self_contained`.
- The existing grid, portal and web tests are updated for the new markup,
  never weakened: each assertion keeps checking the same fact.

Acceptance:
- [ ] The three tests pass. Each is seen failing against a deliberate
  breach (a low-contrast colour, an inflated page, an external font
  link).
