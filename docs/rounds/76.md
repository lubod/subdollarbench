## Items

### F1. An "earlier results" section on the board

- Under the `.46` board, a section **"Earlier results (rules
  2026-10-19.45, not comparable)"**:
  - one compact row per `.45` model that has **no complete `.46`
    result yet**: name (with its colour), median points `/ 76`, spend per
    career, careers;
  - the known-issue line from round 75 F5 (the reference port), in one
    sentence, with a link to the full `.45` report (F2);
  - muted styling (lower contrast than the live board, still WCAG AA),
    no rank numbers, no summary cards: it must not read as part of the
    new ranking.
- When a model finishes its `.46` careers, its row leaves the section.
  When none is left, the section disappears by itself.
- The `.46` board's summary cards and ranks count `.46` careers only.
