## Why

- Rules `2026-11-09.46` (round 75, DECISIONS #30) start an empty board.
  Every model reruns one at a time (the operator: untested models first,
  cheapest first), so the new board fills over days.
- Round 75 F5 keeps the public site on the `.45` bundle until 3 models
  are complete on `.46`. Meanwhile readers see nothing new, and once the
  site switches to `.46` the 6 earlier results would vanish.
- The operator wants the earlier results shown **temporarily**, beside
  the new board, until each model has its `.46` result.

## Items

### F1. An "earlier results" section on the board

- Under the `.46` board, a section **"Earlier results (rules
  2026-10-19.45, not comparable)"**:
  - one compact row per `.45` model that has **no complete `.46`
    result yet**: name (with its colour), median points `/ 76`, spend per
    career, careers;
  - the known-issue line from round 75 F5 (the reference port), in one
    sentence, with a link to the full `.45` report (F2);
  - muted styling (lower contrast than the live board, still WCAG AA),
    no rank numbers, no summary cards: it must not read as part of the
    new ranking.
- When a model finishes its `.46` careers, its row leaves the section.
  When none is left, the section disappears by itself.
- The `.46` board's summary cards and ranks count `.46` careers only.

### F2. The full `.45` report stays reachable

- The export writes the `.45` results page as `results-45.html` (the

Acceptance:

### F3. Publishing with the earlier section

- `publish.sh` (round 75 F5) refuses fewer than 3 complete `.46` models.
  With F1's section present, it accepts **1 or more** complete `.46`
  models: the page then has the new results and the earlier ones,
  clearly separated. With no `.46` model complete, it still refuses.
- The size caps (150 KB per page, round 71) hold with the section and
  `results-45.html`.

Acceptance:
- [ ] `scripts/publish.sh --self-test`: 0 complete `.46` models refuse;
  1 with the earlier section passes; the page sizes stay under the cap
  on a fixture of 10 `.46` + 6 `.45` models.
