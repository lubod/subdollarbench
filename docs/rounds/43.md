## Why

This is the second half of the operator's plan (2026-10-27, "plan a a";
D1 and D2 are in `41.md`). Round 41 removes the callers, and this round deletes what they called.
Round 42 (the TUI) lands before this one, so this round's bar also
covers round 42's `Cargo.lock` change.

- **Size:**
  - 8 retired verifiers in `src/verifier/` (redis, http, proxy, pdf,
    wal, queue, binproto, pubsub), about 15,000 lines;
  - 8 task folders in `tasks/` (all but `sql`), 4 campaigns in
    `campaigns/` (sdb-edge, sdb-http, sdb-kv, sdb-queue);
  - about 300 `TaskType::<retired>` matches, mostly in
    `src/bench/batch_ref.rs` (72), `src/config.rs` (63) and
    `src/pipeline.rs` (59).
- **No bump.** Grading and the candidate's prompts don't change, so
  `RULES_VERSION` stays `.42`. The career chain checks only the rules
  version (`src/main_campaign.rs:493`, `src/campaign.rs:942`), so careers
  continue across the change.
- **The fingerprint changes.** It hashes all of `src/verifier`,
  `tasks/`, `src/bench/` and `src/report/leaderboard.rs`
  (`src/report/graded-files.txt`). That's why this goes before
  calibration (D1): every published cell carries the post-deletion stamp.
- **A graded-path change without a bump** needs the full gate and a new
  20-pass bar before the next published cell (AGENTS.md, "Rules
  version").

The open graded findings come in too, so they share that one bar:
R36-3, R36-5, R37-4, R37-5 and R37-8. Two more disappear with the
proxy code: R37-3 (no live 3-upstream test) and R37-10 (the sdb-edge
fixture's CI flake).

### F4. The graded lists agree; CI's coverage

- **The two lists disagree:**
  - The fingerprint's `src/report/graded-files.txt` hashes `tasks/`,
    `src/bench/` and `src/report/leaderboard.rs`.
  - `scripts/graded-paths.txt`, which sets the gate's scope and the
    flake bar's key, lists none of the three.
  - So an edit to `tasks/sql/` changes the fingerprint but not the
    flake key, and the gate calls it non-graded.
- **The fix:**
  - `graded-paths.txt` covers everything `graded-files.txt` hashes;
  - a test enforces it: every `file`, `dir`, `runtime-file` and
    `runtime-dir` entry is under some graded path.
  - After this round, entries for deleted files are gone from both.
- **CI:**
  - drop the retired-verifier exclusion from `.github/workflows/ci.yml`
    (`--ignore-filename-regex 'src/verifier/(redis|…)\.rs$'`);
  - measure the line coverage on the new CI set and set the ratchet to
    that value rounded down, never below 83 (round 29's rule). Update
    the comment and AGENTS.md's CI paragraph.

Acceptance:
- [ ] Every graded-files entry is covered by graded-paths — test:
  `r43_f4_graded_lists_agree`.
- [ ] CI is green on the round's branch push or on master after the
  merge, with the new ratchet — check: the run id and coverage in the
  notes or the merge record.
