## Why

Rounds 24, 27 and 28 made the stamp cover what grades a run, made the
price basis explicit, and built the flake bar. What's left for the
freeze:

- **Small stamp and label fixes** from the round-28 review (`28.md`):
  - **R28-2:** `graded_hash::collect_dir` (`src/report/graded_hash.rs`)
    ignores a missing directory, so a mistyped `dir` entry hashes as
    nothing.
  - **R28-3:** rules versions are compared as strings
    (`recorded_cap_basis`, `src/report/rules.rs`; the Board header,
    `src/web/index.html` ~:2865).
  - **R28-4:** the portal row reads "cap The $0.99 cell cap is…".
- **R27-6:** F2 of round 27 changed three Gemini cache-write prices. The
  final version's history entry must say so.
- **CI:** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` is `disabled_manually`. Its last
  runs (2026-10-14) failed on clippy. It runs the whole suite and then
  `cargo llvm-cov --fail-under-lines 80`; the bench measured about 70 %
  (`docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` Q6).
- **The final version:** while sdb-sql is in development, bumps are free
  (AGENTS.md, "Rules version"). The freeze ends that.

**Order:**

1. This round lands every code change, including the final version.
2. The reviewer runs the 20-pass flake bar on the merged content
   (`27.md`, D2).
3. The freeze is then declared in a docs-only commit: AGENTS.md (bumps
   are no longer free) and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. That commit touches no
   graded path, so the flake record still applies to it.

If the flake bar finds a flaky proof, the fix is a new round and a new
version, and the freeze waits.

## Operator decisions

- **D1 (CI): what does CI run?**
  - **(a) Fast suite, no coverage gate.** `fmt`, `clippy` and the
    `gate.sh --fast` test set (no live SQL ladder proofs), about 10 min
    on a GitHub runner. Coverage is reported, not enforced.
  - **(b) (a) plus a coverage ratchet.** The gate is the measured line
    coverage today (rounded down), and it can only go up.
  - **(c) As `ci.yml` is now.** The whole suite plus 80 % coverage. It
    fails until coverage work raises the bench by about 10 points, which
    is its own round.
  - Reviewer recommends **(b)**: CI catches what the gate catches,
    without a 40-min Docker run on every push, and coverage can't slide.
- **D2 (version name): what is the final version?**
  - Reviewer recommends `2026-10-19.41`: the next number in the series.
    It keeps the format that the stored rollups and the comparisons use.
- **D3 (GitHub):** CI runs on push. Master is about 130 commits ahead of
  `origin/master` (last push 2026-10-19). The operator decides when to
  push.
