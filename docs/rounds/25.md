## Items

### R22-1. The crash label matches the real crash, not loose text

- `mark_agent_crash` (`src/campaign.rs` ~:1479) fires on `output: 65536`,
  `output limit` or `not valid JSON` anywhere in the console. Its message
  hard-codes "65,536".
- Failure: the agent crashes for another reason after an earlier tool-call
  validation error it recovered from. The stage is then mislabelled as an
  output-limit crash.
- Change:
  - Match the `[TOKENS]` line whose `output` equals that turn's maximum,
    followed by the provider `400` in the same session.
  - Put the real number in the line.

Acceptance:
- [ ] A console with a recovered JSON error and a later unrelated crash is
  not labelled `agent_crash` — test: `<name>`
- [ ] A 32,768-token truncation and its `400` gives a line with 32,768 —
  test: `<name>`

### R22-2. `waiting` stays `waiting` while the need still has its lite

- `ladder_inner` (`src/campaign.rs` ~:2138) counts any failed record of a need as decided.
  So after 11 full fails, ticket 14 reads `skipped` for the whole 11-lite
  attempt. Since F2, that is the normal path.
- Change: a need is decided when it has a failed record **and** either
  has no lite or already has a lite record.
- Optional: a need carried as failed that the catch-up won't retry
  (`catch_up_targets`) is decided at phase start.

Acceptance:
- [ ] Full failed, lite pending: the dependent is `waiting` — test:
  `<name>`
- [ ] Full and lite failed: the dependent is `skipped` — test: `<name>`
