## Items

### F1. A busy transaction is retried for a time budget, with jittered backoff

- `lost_update` and the `transfers` writers retry a busy transaction
  until that transaction has spent **10 s** retrying (the contract's
  "more than 10 s" limit), not a fixed count.
- The pause between tries is random and grows: full jitter on an
  exponential backoff, starting at 1-2 ms and capped near 20 ms, so the
  16 clients don't retry in lockstep. The implementer picks the exact
  curve and states it in the notes. `rand` is already a dependency
  (`Cargo.toml:27`).
- Failure text: `increment {i}: still busy after {s} s ({tries} tries)`,
  and the same for `transfer {i}`.
- A server that answers `ERR busy` forever still fails, about 10 s into
  its first transaction. The readers' `BEGIN` busy loop (`:250-256`)
  keeps its behaviour: it runs until the writers are done.
- Nothing else in the checks changes: the clients, the transaction
  counts, `WRITER_PAUSE`, the 1 s reader window and the invariants.
- The budget is a constant in the grader, never an environment variable.
  Tests may pass a shorter budget through a function parameter.

Acceptance:
