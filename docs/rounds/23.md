## Why

Operator decision, 2026-10-22: the benchmark answers **"what does a
dollar buy"**. Today the turn limits decide, not the money:

- In the Haiku career on `.36`, about 20 sessions ended at the turn
  limit and 2 at the dollar limit (both $0.04-0.06 remainders). The rest
  ended on their own.
- No phase came near $0.99: $0.49, $0.71, $0.71, $0.76.
- A model 10x cheaper gets the same turns, so its low price buys no
  extra work.

**Decisions:**

- (a) The budget counts the **API bill only**, at list price.
- (b) Wall time is a **guard only**, about 8 h per phase.
- The $0.99 per-phase cell cap stays (the "Under-$1" identity).
