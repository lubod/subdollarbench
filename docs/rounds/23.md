## Why

Operator decision, 2026-10-22: the benchmark answers **"what does a
dollar buy"**. Today the turn limits decide, not the money:

- In the Haiku career on `.36`, about 20 sessions ended at the turn
  limit and 2 at the dollar limit (both $0.04-0.06 remainders). The rest
  ended on their own.
- No phase came near $0.99: $0.49, $0.71, $0.71, $0.76.
- A model 10x cheaper gets the same turns, so its low price buys no
  extra work.

**Decisions:**

- (a) The budget counts the **API bill only**, at list price.
- (b) Wall time is a **guard only**, about 8 h per phase.
- The $0.99 per-phase cell cap stays (the "Under-$1" identity).

**Findings:**

- **R23-1 (blocker): a phase's last ticket gets the whole pool, so its
  lite can't run.**
  - For the last undecided ticket, `phase_weight` is its own tier, so
    `pool_budget` returns all the headroom (~:2902, ~:2967).
  - If the full attempt runs to its dollar limit and fails, the cell has
    less than `reserve + floor` left. `cap_allows` at ~:3046 then refuses
    the lite.
  - Tickets 15 and 20 are last in their phases and both have lites
    (0.4×5 = 2.0 and 0.4×7 = 2.8 points). With D1, ending on dollars is
    the normal case, so these lites would usually never run.
  - A related skew: a ticket whose `needs` already failed in this phase
    keeps its tier in the denominator until it is reached and skipped.
    For example, 11 fails (full and lite), and 14's tier 5 still dilutes
    12 and 13. The money flows on to the last ticket.
  - Fix:
    - In `runnable_tier_sum`, leave out a stage that has a need with an
      outcome of `Failed` or `Skipped`. Within a phase that is final,
      because the catch-up runs only at phase start.
    - Give a ticket whose lite has not run yet the weight
      `tier × (1 + lite.weight)`, so the full attempt's share leaves the
      lite its part.
    - Apply both in the full, lite and catch-up paths.
  - Tests:
    - the last ticket's full attempt gets less than the headroom when it
      has a lite;
    - the last ticket's lite runs after a full attempt that spent its
      whole share;
    - a ticket with a failed need does not dilute the ones before it.
- **R23-2 (blocker): the Board launcher still says 150 min.**
  - `src/web/index.html` :1190 has `id="campaignWall" value="150"`, and
    the form posts `wall_min: 150`. A cell launched from the Board gets
    the old wall, although D4 acceptance says "480 everywhere".
  - The "resolved:" line (:2934) prints `--timeout-min 20`.
  - Fix: default the input to 480, print 120, and add a smoke test for
    both.
- **R23-3 (next → round 24): the recorded cost prefers the live
  key-usage delta to list price.**
  - `resolve_api_cost` (`src/pipeline.rs` ~:5945,
    `src/cost/calculator.rs` :133) takes the OpenRouter key-usage delta
    when it moves. That is the provider's actual charge (provider routing,
    promos), not the list price that decision (a) names.
  - The in-session stop uses the session's `usage.cost.total`, which is
    also the provider's own number.
  - It fits round 24's price snapshot: price every attempt from the
    snapshot and record the live delta for audit only.
- **R23-4 (note):** an operator cancel is recorded as `crash`
  (`src/sandbox/omp.rs` :866, :1034). It's rare and display only;
  accepted for now.
