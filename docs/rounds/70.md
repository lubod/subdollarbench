### F2. The phone board: names and the agreement strip (R69-3, R69-4)

- **Names:** at 390 px the summary cards break model names mid-word
  (`deepseek/de` `epseek-v4.1-...`), and the board's 118 px name column
  can't hold `deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017`. Names break only after `/` or
  `-` (a `<wbr>` after each); no `break-all` or `anywhere` on names.
- **Agreement:** the ticket-agreement strip is hidden on a phone
  (`.col-agree { display: none; }`). It must stay reachable there: shown
  under the name on a phone, or repeated inside `<details>`.
- The phone layout keeps rounds 65/66's rule: no sideways scroll for
  rank, name, points, perf and spend.

Acceptance:
- [ ] Test `r70_f2_card_name_breaks`: names carry `<wbr>` after `/` and
  `-`; the name CSS has no `break-all` or `anywhere`.
- [ ] Test `r70_f2_agreement_on_phone`: under the phone media query, the
  agreement strip is in an element that is not `display: none`.
- [ ] 390 px screenshots of the real board, collapsed and with one model
  expanded, in the notes.
