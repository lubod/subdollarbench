## Why

`GRADED_FILES` (`src/report/rules.rs` ~:734) lists only the retired
tasks. For sdb-sql, the only active campaign, the stamp moves only when
`RULES_VERSION` is bumped by hand.

None of these are covered:

- `campaigns/sdb-sql/campaign.json` and the stage prompts;
- `src/verifier/sql/*`;
- the scoring in `src/campaign.rs` (tier points, lite weight, catch-up
  0.4x, `CATCH_UP_SLOTS` ~:2522) and, since round 23, the money rules:
  `CELL_BUDGET_USD`, `TURN_GUARD` (~:1947), the cap reserve and floor
  (~:357), and the pool weights `stage_weight` / `runnable_weight_sum`
  (~:393);
- the late checks in `src/pipeline.rs`;
- the candidate-facing text: the directives in `src/sandbox/omp.rs`
  and `src/sandbox/opencode.rs`, and the dollar line `session_budget_line`
  (`src/pipeline.rs` ~:1316).

This was the old roadmap item "campaign spec + stage prompts + suites join
GRADED_FILES", which was never done. It matters at the freeze: without
it, a silent change to a prompt or a grader goes unnoticed.

## Implementer notes

- `src/report/rules.rs` now hashes the static sdb-sql inputs
