## Why

`GRADED_FILES` (`src/report/rules.rs` ~:701) lists only the retired
tasks. For sdb-sql, the only active campaign, the stamp moves only when
`RULES_VERSION` is bumped by hand.

None of these are covered:

- `campaigns/sdb-sql/campaign.json` and the stage prompts;
- `src/verifier/sql/*`;
- the scoring in `src/campaign.rs` (tier points, lite weight, catch-up
  0.4x, slots);
- the late checks in `src/pipeline.rs`;
- the candidate-facing directives in `src/sandbox/omp.rs`.

This was the old roadmap item "campaign spec + stage prompts + suites join
GRADED_FILES", which was never done. It matters at the freeze: without
it, a silent change to a prompt or a grader goes unnoticed.

## Items

### R1. Fingerprint the sdb-sql grading inputs

- Add:
  - `campaigns/sdb-sql/campaign.json`;
  - every stage `prompt.md`, full and lite (by glob, so a new stage is
    included automatically);
  - `src/verifier/sql/*.rs`.
- Move the scoring constants and the candidate-facing prompt text out of
  `campaign.rs`, `pipeline.rs` and `omp.rs`, into small files that can be
  listed. Don't hash whole `campaign.rs`/`pipeline.rs`: GUI or logging
  edits would churn the stamp.
- Remove the retired tasks from the list, or keep them, and say which
  and why.
