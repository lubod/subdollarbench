## Why

Mimo `.51` career 0 (2026-11-20/21, `runs/.grid-v51-mimo-ledger.tsv`):
phases 1-2 scored 10/10 and 12/12; phase 3 scored 4/22 (ticket 11
passed, 12-15 failed) and phase 4 0/32. Every failure from ticket 12 on
reads `broke ticket 4 ... [upgrade] drift: read "SELECT ... FROM
s87da1a20d61d96e4_slot ..." answered "ERR no_table no such table"`.

The reviewer's reading of the run:

- Phase 2 ended with a 688 KB store holding every sentinel table; the
  phase-3 store and its rollback snapshot are 24 KB without them.
- In ticket 11's verification (phase 3,
  `campaign_sdb-sql_openrouter_xiaomi_mimo-v2.6-flash_20261120_112540_w1`)
  the upgrade check read the stage-4 sentinel at 13:20:56 UTC; then the
  crash ladder ran on a **scratch copy** (`src/pipeline.rs:171`), the
  transaction checks ran on the **real store**, including a kill with a
  transaction open (`src/pipeline.rs:1962-1985`,
  `kill_with_open_transaction`, `src/verifier/sql/txn.rs:216`), the perf
  gate ran on a fresh scratch store and restored onto the real one, and
  the stage-11 sentinel was written. The next ticket's first
  verification found the stage-4 tables gone. The candidate's recovery
  after the kill (or a restart) discarded most of its store: a real
  durability bug in the model's code.
- The rollback snapshot is taken at the start of the next attempt
  (`src/main_campaign.rs:1106-1145`), i.e. **after** the damage, so every
  later rollback restored the broken store ("[ROLLBACK] sdb-sql store
  restored from its snapshot" before tickets 12 lite, 13, 14, 15, ...),
  and tickets 12-20 failed whatever the model did.
- The loss was charged to ticket 4 (persistence) at every later ticket,
  not to the check that exposed it.

The model deserves to lose points for losing committed data on a kill;
it should not lose the rest of its career for it. Deepseek `.50`
survived the same check (its recovery is correct).

## Items

### F1. The transaction kill test runs on a scratch copy

Like the crash ladder, `kill_with_open_transaction` (and any other late
check that kills or restarts the candidate destructively on purpose)
runs on a scratch copy of the store, then the candidate is restored onto
the real store through `restart_after_scenario`. List every late check
and the store it uses in the notes; any destructive one not on a scratch
copy is moved or justified there.

Acceptance:
- [x] The kill-with-open-transaction check leaves the real store's
      files byte-identical — test: `r95_f1_txn_kill_on_a_scratch_copy`
      (seen failing)
- [x] A candidate whose recovery drops old data after a kill still fails
      stage 6's check (the scratch copy shows the loss) — same or a
      sibling test

### F2. The rollback snapshot is a verified store

The snapshot a rollback restores is only taken from a store that passed
the upgrade (sentinel) check at the end of a passed ticket's
verification; if the store fails it, the snapshot stays at the last
verified one. A store loss can therefore cost the ticket that caused it,
never the rest of the career.

Acceptance:
- [x] A store damaged after its ticket's checks: the next attempt's
      rollback restores the last verified store and the sentinels read —
      test: `r95_f2_rollback_restores_a_verified_store` (seen failing)
- [x] An undamaged run snapshots as today

### F3. A store loss is charged where it happened

At the end of every verification (after the destructive late checks),
the upgrade sentinels are read once more on the real store. A failure
there fails **this** ticket with the check that preceded it named
(`store lost after <check>`), attributed to that check's ticket, instead
of surfacing as `broke ticket 4` at the next one.

Acceptance:
- [x] A candidate that loses data on the kill check fails the current
      ticket with `store lost after txn-kill` (or the name of the check)
      — test: `r95_f3_store_loss_charged_where_it_happened` (seen failing)

### The design (advisor, plan / before_approach)

One consultation after the design: the stage-6 checks on a scratch copy
with the sentinels read there; `store_verified` gating the snapshot; the
end-of-check sentinel reads; the replay set. The advice: keep the restore
on every exit path (the crash-ladder closure pattern); default
`store_verified` false; snapshot the store and sentinel pair together;
confirm the cleanup stop mode; extend the replay set rather than fork
round 91's test. Followed: the exit paths match `crash_ladder`, the pair
is copied and restored as one unit, the snapshot is taken after

F4 proof (see the finding below). Not followed: none.

### F1. The transaction kill test runs on a scratch copy

`txn_scenario` (`src/pipeline.rs:464`) quiesces the real store with
ticket 4's graceful stop, copies it to `sql-data-txn`, runs isolation and
`kill_with_open_transaction` there, reads the older sentinels on the copy
after the kill, and restores the candidate onto the real store on every
exit path (`restart_after_scenario("txn", ...)`; a failed restore is
ticket 4's contract). The crash ladder's copy now also uses the graceful
quiesce (a kill to take a copy is not needed and would force the recovery
on the real store for nothing). `r95_f1_txn_kill_on_a_scratch_copy`
starts the compact reference on a real store with a sentinel, calls the
check, and compares every file of the real store byte for byte; the copy
is gone and the sentinel still reads. Seen failing at `9250f0d` with the
bind rewrite removed ("the transaction kill changed the real store's
files"). `r95_f3` (below) is the sibling proof: the recovery's loss on
the copy fails stage 6.

### F2. The rollback snapshot is a verified store

The verification reports `store_verified` (every stage passed, so the
closing upgrade read passed too; F3 fails the ticket otherwise), and only
then does the attempt write `sql-data.snap` + `sql-sentinels.snap`
(`verifier::sql::store`). A rollback restores the pair and keeps it for a
further retry; a failed ticket leaves the last verified snapshot in
place. The start-of-attempt snapshot is gone (a fresh career has none
until its first pass; a chained phase inherits the predecessor's pair
(`src/main_campaign.rs:683` `copy_store`). `r95_f2_rollback_restores_a_verified_store`
snapshots a reference store with a sentinel, damages it (the table
dropped), snapshots with `verified=false` (the pair stays), restores, and
the sentinels read; an undamaged snapshot is the store's own bytes. Seen
failing at `9250f0d` with the `verified` gate removed: the damaged store
replaces the snapshot and the restored sentinel does not read.

### F3. A store loss is charged where it happened

Sentinels are read (`store_after_check`, `src/pipeline.rs:416`) after the differential suite and after every late
check that restarts the candidate on the real store (crash ladder,
txn-kill, index-perf, memory, disk-full, security, pgwire), and once more
after the last late check. A drift fails this ticket, names the check
(`store lost after <check>`) and the ticket the lost data belongs to
(`sentinel from stage N`), instead of the next ticket's `broke ticket N`.
`r95_f3_store_loss_charged_where_it_happened` writes sentinel-4 through a
passed ticket-4 verification, patches the reference so a recovery that
finds an open transaction drops the table, and runs ticket 6 (stages 0-4
+ 6, no crash ladder): stage 6 fails with `store lost after txn-kill:
sentinel from stage 4: ...`, and a candidate started on the real store
still answers the sentinel. Seen failing at `9250f0d` with the copy's
sentinel read removed (stage 6 passes).
