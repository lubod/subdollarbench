## Why

Mimo `.51` career 0 (2026-11-20/21, `runs/.grid-v51-mimo-ledger.tsv`):
phases 1-2 scored 10/10 and 12/12; phase 3 scored 4/22 (ticket 11
passed, 12-15 failed) and phase 4 0/32. Every failure from ticket 12 on
reads `broke ticket 4 ... [upgrade] drift: read "SELECT ... FROM
s87da1a20d61d96e4_slot ..." answered "ERR no_table no such table"`.

The reviewer's reading of the run:

- Phase 2 ended with a 688 KB store holding every sentinel table; the
  phase-3 store and its rollback snapshot are 24 KB without them.
- In ticket 11's verification (phase 3,
  `campaign_sdb-sql_openrouter_xiaomi_mimo-v2.6-flash_20261120_112540_w1`)
  the upgrade check read the stage-4 sentinel at 13:20:56 UTC; then the
  crash ladder ran on a **scratch copy** (`src/pipeline.rs:171`), the
  transaction checks ran on the **real store**, including a kill with a
  transaction open (`src/pipeline.rs:1962-1985`,
  `kill_with_open_transaction`, `src/verifier/sql/txn.rs:216`), the perf
  gate ran on a fresh scratch store and restored onto the real one, and
  the stage-11 sentinel was written. The next ticket's first
  verification found the stage-4 tables gone. The candidate's recovery
  after the kill (or a restart) discarded most of its store: a real
  durability bug in the model's code.
- The rollback snapshot is taken at the start of the next attempt
  (`src/main_campaign.rs:1106-1145`), i.e. **after** the damage, so every
  later rollback restored the broken store ("[ROLLBACK] sdb-sql store
  restored from its snapshot" before tickets 12 lite, 13, 14, 15, ...),
  and tickets 12-20 failed whatever the model did.
- The loss was charged to ticket 4 (persistence) at every later ticket,
  not to the check that exposed it.

The model deserves to lose points for losing committed data on a kill;
it should not lose the rest of its career for it. Deepseek `.50`
survived the same check (its recovery is correct).

## Items

### F1. The transaction kill test runs on a scratch copy

Like the crash ladder, `kill_with_open_transaction` (and any other late
check that kills or restarts the candidate destructively on purpose)
runs on a scratch copy of the store, then the candidate is restored onto
the real store through `restart_after_scenario`. List every late check
and the store it uses in the notes; any destructive one not on a scratch
copy is moved or justified there.

Acceptance:
- [ ] The kill-with-open-transaction check leaves the real store's
      files byte-identical — test: `r95_f1_txn_kill_on_a_scratch_copy`
      (seen failing)
- [ ] A candidate whose recovery drops old data after a kill still fails
      stage 6's check (the scratch copy shows the loss) — same or a
      sibling test

### F2. The rollback snapshot is a verified store

The snapshot a rollback restores is only taken from a store that passed
the upgrade (sentinel) check at the end of a passed ticket's
verification; if the store fails it, the snapshot stays at the last
verified one. A store loss can therefore cost the ticket that caused it,
never the rest of the career.

Acceptance:
- [ ] A store damaged after its ticket's checks: the next attempt's
      rollback restores the last verified store and the sentinels read —
      test: `r95_f2_rollback_restores_a_verified_store` (seen failing)
- [ ] An undamaged run snapshots as today

### F3. A store loss is charged where it happened

At the end of every verification (after the destructive late checks),
the upgrade sentinels are read once more on the real store. A failure
there fails **this** ticket with the check that preceded it named
(`store lost after <check>`), attributed to that check's ticket, instead
of surfacing as `broke ticket 4` at the next one.

Acceptance:
- [ ] A candidate that loses data on the kill check fails the current
      ticket with `store lost after txn-kill` (or the name of the check)
      — test: `r95_f3_store_loss_charged_where_it_happened` (seen failing)
