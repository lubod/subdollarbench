## Items

### F1. Turns scale with the ticket's budget

A ticket's turn limit is `max(stage.max_turns, ceil(stage.max_turns ×
budget_usd / 0.20))`: 200 turns per $0.20 allotted, never fewer than
the stage's own limit. So an ordinary ticket keeps 200, and a $0.97
catch-up gets 970. The reference amount ($0.20) is one named constant
beside `OFFICIAL_CELL_TIMEOUT_MIN`, used for full attempts, lite
attempts, catch-ups and resumes alike (a resume's turns follow its own
budget top-up).

Acceptance:
- [x] $0.09 gives 200, $0.20 gives 200, $0.41 gives 410, $0.97 gives 970,
      for full, lite and catch-up attempts alike — test:
      `r86_f1_turns_scale_with_budget` (the operator's "300" change
      amends the examples to 200/300/615/1,455; the test uses those)
- [x] The catch-up attempt in the campaign loop receives the scaled limit
      (not the stage's raw 200) — test: `r86_f1_catch_up_gets_scaled_turns`
- [x] Each seen failing

### F2. The ticket time-out scales the same way, inside the phase wall

A ticket's time-out is `max(120 min, 120 min × budget_usd / 0.20)`,
never more than the phase's remaining wall time (480 minutes in all,
`--wall-min`). So the extra turns are not simply lost to the 120-minute
time-out.

      remaining wall; a ticket near the phase's end gets only what is
      left — test: `r86_f2_timeout_scales_within_the_wall`

## Changes after open

- **2026-11-15, operator: "300"** (before the implementer started). F1's
  rate is **300 turns per $0.20 (1,500 turns per dollar)**, not 200, so
  that the dollar binds before the turns even for the cheapest models
  (deepseek buys about 1,300 turns per dollar at its median $0.00076 a
  turn; with 1,000 per dollar it would still stop at about 77 % of its
  money). The floor stays the stage's own 200. F1 becomes
  `max(stage.max_turns, ceil(300 × budget_usd / 0.20))`, and its
  acceptance examples become: $0.09 gives 200, $0.20 gives 300, $0.41
  gives 615, $0.97 gives 1,455. F2 (time) is unchanged: 120 minutes per
  $0.20, floor 120, inside the phase's remaining wall (deepseek's 200-turn
  tickets took 29-67 minutes, so $0.20 of its turns fits in 120). F3's
  example becomes `Max turns: 1455` for a $0.97 ticket.
