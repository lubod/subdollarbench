## Why

- **The reviewer's read of `/portal/`** (2026-11-06, the v2 grid at 52/60).
  The per-career numbers match the ledger: points, $ and perf. But:
  - **B1:** the header's "Best points 71.0" is the $0 rehearsal (the
    reference server). The best real career is 70.0.
  - **B2:** a haiku career reads `57.599999999999994/76`.
  - **B3:** old `.42`/`.44` cells (2026-10-29/31) are listed among
    "unfinished careers" as "phase 1 of 4". One shows perf 2.02×.
  - **M3:** nothing says the grid is still running. Three models have 2
    of 3 careers, and with 2 careers the "median" is the worse one.
  - **H1-H5:** campaign ids as names; the rules, SQLite version and cap
    repeated in every Points cell; "Reached", "Reg-free" and "Reg-free /
    $" unexplained; a per-row "Updated"; the median career shown twice.
- **R63-2:** the GUI Board ranks the $0 rehearsal as #1
  (`src/web/index.html`, `renderBoard`).
- **The operator wants duration, turns and tokens.** Every ticket's
  `s*/manifest.json` already records `tokens.{prompt,cached,completion,
  total}_tokens`, and `campaign.json` holds `started_at`/`finished_at` and
  per-stage `turns`. 84-97% of tokens are cached input, so a single
  "tokens" number misleads.
- No graded path: `src/report/grid.rs`, `src/report/portal.rs` and
  `src/web/` are outside `scripts/graded-paths.txt`. The provider-usage
  mapping that leaves `prompt_tokens` at 0 for the Anthropic and OpenAI
  routes is in the harness (graded). It goes to round 65 with R63-3.

### F6. Variance between a model's careers (operator; D1)

- **The dot strips.** In the model rows, each of points, perf, $,
  duration, turns and tokens out shows every career as a dot and the
  median as a diamond, on a fixed scale per column (points 0-76; perf
  log, 0.1-10×). It is an inline SVG with the values in its title. The
  number next to it is the median.
- **Rank stability (D1).** The rank column shows the model's most likely
  rank and its share, for example `#1 (61%)`. Hover shows the whole
  distribution. The share is computed exactly, over every combination of
  one finished career per model: 3^5 = 243 when the grid is complete.
  Combinations are ranked by the board order.
  - It replaces the `N=` groups.
  - With more than 100,000 combinations, it says "too many to
    enumerate" and falls back to the board order. There's no sampling,
    so a page is reproducible.
- **Ticket agreement** (the expanded model view). The ladder squares
  are shaded by how many careers passed each ticket (3/3, 2/3, 1/3,
  0/3), with `agree 18/20` and the differing tickets listed.
- **The results page** gets:
  - the head-to-head table: the share of career pairs in which model A's
    career outscores model B's, by points;
  - per metric, the median within-model spread against the span of the
    model medians, and their ratio, labelled as the publishing bar's
    variance check (`TODO_CAMPAIGNS.md`, "The bar").

Acceptance:
- [x] Rank stability on a fixture of 3 models × 3 careers matches a
  hand-counted case: 27 combinations, with the shares listed in the
  test. A model with identical careers gets 100% of its rank. Test:
  `r64_f6_rank_stability_exact`. Seen failing with a sampled or
  first-career-only shortcut.
- [x] Ticket agreement on a fixture: 18/20, with the two differing ids.
  Test: `r64_f6_ticket_agreement`.
- [x] Head-to-head and within/between on the reviewer's 2026-11-06 data

  numbers.
- [x] Each dot strip renders as SVG with one dot per career and a title
  holding the values. Test: `r64_f6_dot_strip_svg`.
