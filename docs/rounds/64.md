### F5. Duration, turns and tokens (operator)

- **Per career:**
  - **Duration:** the sum of the phases' wall time
    (`finished_at - started_at`), shown as `5.4 h`. The tooltip shows the
    agent's share (the sum of `stages[].duration_seconds`).
  - **Turns:** the sum of `stages[].turns`.
  - **Tokens:** from every `s*/manifest.json`, summed. It shows
    `out 1.7M · in 5.9M (+154M cached)`.
    - Fresh input is `total - cached - completion`, because
      `prompt_tokens` is 0 on some routes (round 65 fixes the source).
    - The tooltip gives all four numbers and the cache share.
- **Per model:** the medians of the same three.
- They are display only: no ranking change.
- A career whose manifests lack `tokens` shows `—`.

Acceptance:
- [ ] On a fixture career with two manifests (one with

  Test: `r64_f5_duration_turns_tokens`.
- [ ] The notes give the real export's numbers for deepseek c0: about

### F6. Variance between a model's careers (operator; D1)

- **The dot strips.** In the model rows, each of points, perf, $,
  duration, turns and tokens out shows every career as a dot and the
  median as a diamond, on a fixed scale per column (points 0-76; perf
  log, 0.1-10×). It is an inline SVG with the values in its title. The
  number next to it is the median.
- **Rank stability (D1).** The rank column shows the model's most likely
  rank and its share, for example `#1 (61%)`. Hover shows the whole
  distribution. The share is computed exactly, over every combination of
  one finished career per model: 3^5 = 243 when the grid is complete.
  Combinations are ranked by the board order.
  - It replaces the `N=` groups.
  - With more than 100,000 combinations, it says "too many to
    enumerate" and falls back to the board order. There's no sampling,
    so a page is reproducible.
- **Ticket agreement** (the expanded model view). The ladder squares
  are shaded by how many careers passed each ticket (3/3, 2/3, 1/3,
  0/3), with `agree 18/20` and the differing tickets listed.
- **The results page** gets:
  - the head-to-head table: the share of career pairs in which model A's
    career outscores model B's, by points;
  - per metric, the median within-model spread against the span of the
    model medians, and their ratio, labelled as the publishing bar's
    variance check (`TODO_CAMPAIGNS.md`, "The bar").

Acceptance:
- [ ] Rank stability on a fixture of 3 models × 3 careers matches a
  hand-counted case: 27 combinations, with the shares listed in the
  test. A model with identical careers gets 100% of its rank. Test:
  `r64_f6_rank_stability_exact`. Seen failing with a sampled or
  first-career-only shortcut.
- [ ] Ticket agreement on a fixture: 18/20, with the two differing ids.
  Test: `r64_f6_ticket_agreement`.
- [ ] Head-to-head and within/between on the reviewer's 2026-11-06 data
  (the 12 finished careers) reproduce the notes' table: deepseek beats
  mimo in 67% of pairs; points spread 7.0 within against 37.6 between.
  Test: `r64_f6_head_to_head_and_spread`, on a fixture copy of those
  numbers.
- [ ] Each dot strip renders as SVG with one dot per career and a title
  holding the values. Test: `r64_f6_dot_strip_svg`.
