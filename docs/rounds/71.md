## Why

- **Size (R70-2):** the real board is 137 KB with 18 careers (6
  models). The 150 KB cap of `r69_f4_page_size` is tested on a 15-career
  fixture only, and nothing enforces it on the real export. The grid
  adds 9 careers (luna-pro, mimo-pro, hy4), so the board passes the cap
  around 21-24 careers. On the 11-08 export:
  - 637 ticket squares, 630 of them with a `title`;
  - each model's agreement strip is emitted twice (the row, and
    `<div class="agree agree-details">` inside `<details>`, round 70
    F2: about 9.7 KB);
  - 36 dot strips (`<svg class="dots">`).
- **Padding (R70-3):** the fingerprint note under "What it measured"
  sits flush against the card's left edge (`.measured { padding: 0; }`).
- **Colours (R70-4):** `palette()` (`src/report/grid.rs:2080`) has 8
  Okabe-Ito colours: two oranges (`#E69F00`, `#D55E00`), two blues
  (`#56B4E9`, `#0072B2`), a yellow, a green, a pink, a grey. On today's
  board xiaomi and minimax read as the same orange on the scatter, and
  deepseek and qwen as the same blue. After hy4 there are **9 models**,
  more than the palette has, so colours would repeat outright.

## Items

### F1. A size budget that holds at 30 careers (R70-2)

- **One agreement strip per model.** It's rendered once. On a phone it
  stays reachable without a second copy: for example, the row's cell
  becomes a full-width block under the row (CSS on the same element), or
  the strip lives only in `<details>` and the desktop column links to
  it. Your choice; describe it in the notes.
- **Shorter repeated markup.** Ticket squares and dot strips are the
  bulk. Shorten what repeats per square: class names, inline styles and
  `title` text. Keep every number reachable (the round 69 constraint
  holds): a square's title may become terser, for example `t5 2/3`
  instead of `ticket 5 · passed by 2/3`, if the legend explains the
  form.
- **Budget:** each page at most **150 KB with 30 careers across 10
  models**. This is a new fixture with realistic content: all 4 phases,
  21 tickets per career, every model expanded.
- **Enforced on the real export:** `scripts/publish.sh` refuses an
  export whose `index.html` or `results.html` is over 150 KB, and says
  which page and its size. (`publish.sh` isn't a graded path.)

### F3. At least 10 colours, and neighbours that differ (R70-4)

- The palette has **at least 10** colours, each distinct from the others
  on the dark background, and meeting round 69's contrast floor for

  square, triangle, diamond, ...) and a dash pattern on the survival
  chart, assigned in board order. The legends show the shape or dash.
- Two models from the same hue family (two blues, two oranges) never
  share a marker shape.
