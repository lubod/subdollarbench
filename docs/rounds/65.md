## Why

- **R63-3: the grid's credit outage** (`63.md`, "The grid's credit
  outage").
  - From 2026-11-04 20:51 CET (19:51 UTC), OpenRouter answered every
    call with:

    ```
    [STDERR] 402 This request requires more credits, or fewer max_tokens. You requested up to 65536 tokens, but can only afford 44804. To increase, visit https://openrouter.ai/...
    ```

  - The harness recorded 32 phases as `ok` with 0 points (or fewer). The
    grid ran on; they were voided by hand (`runs/void-402-20261104/`).
  - **The machinery exists.** `classify_billing_error`
    (`src/sandbox/omp.rs:244`) is called on every stderr and session line
    (`:910`, `:1302`). A trip sets `billing_tripped`, and the run ends as
    a provider failure (`:1030-1045`). A phase that doesn't stop `ok`
    makes `scripts/grid.sh` touch its STOP file (`:372-375`).
  - **The matcher missed the wording.** Its text arm matches a status
    line or the phrases `payment required`, `insufficient credit`,
    `credit exhausted` and `out of credit` (`:321-331`). Bare `402`
    digits are deliberately ignored (`:230-233`). OpenRouter's
    `requires more credits ... can only afford` matches none of them.
  - The cost: 32 phases rerun, and $19.91 plus a second top-up, all
    found after the fact.
- **The account balance is never checked.** The grid's `--cap` counts
  list-price spend in its own ledger. The account (shared by every key)
  had about $18 left against a projected $27. The reviewer's stopgap
  `runs/grid-402-guard.sh` held the rerun once, at $4.99. It isn't part
  of the repo.
- **New (reviewer, 2026-11-06): `scripts/publish.sh` computes a stale
  bar key.**
  - Its own `flake_key` (`scripts/publish.sh:40-44`) hashes
    `git ls-tree` over `graded-paths.txt` directly. It doesn't skip
    `tests.rs`, and it passes round 61's `exclude-name`/`keep` data lines
    as pathspecs.
  - So it asked for `flake-7dd4ffb89fbd.txt`, while `flake-bar.sh --key`
    gives `a249e9c32c47`. Every publish is refused.
  - Round 61 R61-5 moved every other reader to `scripts/graded-filter.sh`
    and missed this one. Today's manual bundle needed `SDB_BAR_RECORD`.
- **Tokens: `prompt_tokens` is 0 on the Anthropic and OpenAI routes.**
  Every `s*/manifest.json` of haiku and luna reads `prompt_tokens: 0`,
  while `total - cached - completion` is 1.7-2.4M per career. The usage
  parser (`src/sandbox/omp.rs:1373-1394`, `:1435`) misses those routes'
  input field. The round-64 page derives fresh input instead.
