## Why

Three problems on 2026-11-08/09 left clear traces in data the harness
already had, and were found by hand, hours or days late
(`docs/DECISIONS.md` #28, #29):

  found the next day, after $8 and a published result.

  manual pre-check, after the grid was armed.
- **The reference server (`ref-sql`) drops out** for short spells during
  agent sessions: 19-27 % of the OpenAI models' reference queries were
  refused, 1-4 % of the others'. Never noticed.

Round 68 adds the pre-grid model check with a tool call (F3) and a
post-phase provider-fault check (F5). This round adds the rest: health
numbers for every phase, a live check that stops a bad phase early, a
canary before the other lanes start, an audit that gates publishing,
and the `ref-sql` fix.

### F2. A live check, and a canary before the other lanes

- **Live check:** while a phase runs, `grid.sh` reads its health numbers
  every 2 minutes. It **stops the phase** (the cell is killed, the run is
  marked `provider_fault` and hidden, the grid holds with the reason)
  when, after at least 20 turns:
  - tool errors are over 2 % of turns and at least 5; or
  - empty responses are over 5 % of turns and at least 5.
  It **warns** (log line and GUI, no stop) when the median turn is over
  180 s or `ref-sql` refusals pass 10 % of reference queries.
- **Canary:** in a paid grid, lane 1 starts alone. The other lanes start
  only when its live check has passed 30 turns cleanly; if it stops, no
  other lane starts.
- Round 68 F5's post-phase check stays as the backstop.
