**What publishing needs and doesn't have today:**

  "Publish") don't exist: points against dollars (Pareto), the survival
  curve P(ticket ≥ i), `$` per ticket, and regression-free rates.
- **The variance check** ("the spread between models must be larger
  than the spread between one model's own careers, and the ranking must
  hold across reruns") has no tool.
- **Nothing deploys** the export.

**What exists to build on:**

- Phase cells carry career totals (`CareerRollup`,
  `src/campaign.rs:2358`).
- The console already ranks a phase as its career (`BoardSortKey::of`,
  `src/campaign.rs:2070-2078`; `board_order`, `:2098`).
- `survival()` exists per rollup (`:2481`).
- The phases are 0-6, 7-10, 11-15 and 16-20
  (`campaigns/sdb-sql/campaign.json:649`).
- The portal has a secret redactor (`secret_patterns`,
  `src/report/portal.rs:2081`).

**One ranking** (`gui-spec.md` §4.1): the portal *uses*
`BoardSortKey`/`board_order` and `is_current_rules`. It doesn't
re-implement them, and doesn't edit `src/campaign.rs`, which is graded.

## Operator decisions

  - Rejected: (b) a gate: publishing waits until no adjacent pair overlaps, which
    means more careers.

### F2. The results page

A second page, `results.html`, linked from the board. Static HTML,
charts as inline SVG generated in Rust, no JavaScript and no new crate.
Every chart has a table with its numbers next to it.

- **Points against dollars:** one dot per career, coloured by model, with
  the Pareto frontier through the non-dominated careers. Axes are career
  `$` (0 to the career cap of 4 × $0.99) and career points (0 to 76).
- **The survival curve:** P(ticket ≥ i) per model, for i = 0…20,
  averaged over its finished careers. It uses each phase cell's
  per-ticket pass and the same rule as `survival()`.
- **`$` per ticket:** career `$` divided by tickets passed, per model:
  median, min and max.
- **The regression-free rate:** regression-free tickets divided by
  tickets passed, per model.
- **What it measured,** at the top: rules version and fingerprint,
  SQLite version, sandbox image, harness and effort, the cap ($0.99 per
  phase, 4 phases), the grid's dates, the number of careers, and a link
  to the methodology (`docs/PROPOSAL-sdb-sql.md` on GitHub).

Acceptance:
- [ ] On F1's fixture, the frontier contains exactly the non-dominated
  careers — test: `r44_f2_pareto_frontier`.
- [ ] Survival, `$` per ticket and the regression-free rate match
  values computed by hand for the fixture — test: `r44_f2_metrics`.
- [ ] The page names the rules version, the fingerprint and the image —
  test: `r44_f2_results_page_names_what_it_measured`.

### F4. Deploy to the VPS: `scripts/publish.sh`

- **Exports** the portal into a new temporary directory, then checks:
  1. every ranked cell is on the current rules and all of them share one
     fingerprint;
  2. the flake record for that graded content says `RESULT: PASS`;
  3. the export passes the secret scan (`secret_patterns`) with zero
     hits, and contains no `runs/` logs or workspaces;
  4. its size is printed.
- **Pushes** with `rsync -a --delete` to `SDB_PUBLISH_TARGET` from
  `.env` (for example `deploy@host:/var/www/subdollarbench/`). It prints
  `SDB_PUBLISH_URL` at the end.
- **A dry run by default** (`rsync -n`, with the file list). `--yes`
  pushes.
- **Never pushes** without the target set. The script doesn't print
  values from `.env` other than the target and the URL.
- **`README.md`, "Publishing":** the two `.env` keys, a minimal nginx
  `server` block for a static directory, TLS with certbot, and the
  command. The VPS setup is the operator's; the repo only documents it.

Acceptance:
- [ ] `scripts/publish.sh --self-test`, with a fixture export and a
  local target directory, covers:
  - a mixed-fingerprint export: refused;
  - a missing bar: refused;
  - a planted `sk-or-…` string: refused;
  - a dry run: lists the files and copies nothing;
  - `--yes`: copies.

  Check: the output, quoted in the notes.

**Blockers:**

- **R44-1: the fingerprint check can't fail.**
  - `export` writes `ranked_fingerprints` as the *current binary's*
    `fingerprint()` (`src/report/grid.rs:1120`), not the cells'.
    `publish.sh` then compares that list with the same value, so it
    always passes.
  - Its self-test only feeds a hand-written `grid.json`, so it can't
    notice. That's the same kind of tautology as R37-2.
  - The cells do record their stamp: each ticket manifest's
    `rules_version` is the full fingerprint (`src/pipeline.rs:6017`).
  - **Fix:**
    - `grid.json` lists the distinct fingerprints read from the ranked
      careers' ticket manifests.
    - The results page shows the recorded one, and says so when it
      differs from the binary's.
    - `publish.sh` refuses more than one, or one that isn't the
      binary's.
  - **Test:** a fixture with two careers whose manifests carry different
    stamps. It goes through the real exporter and then `publish.sh`'s
    check, and is refused.
- **R44-2: the "survival" curve isn't a survival curve.**
  - `CareerCell::survival` (`:72`) scores ticket i as passed(i) and
    passed(i−1), and the page labels the average "P(ticket ≥ i)"
    (`:1078`).
  - That is not monotonic: a model that fails ticket 3 but passes 4 and
    5 scores 0 at 3 and 4, then 1 at 5.
  - The cause is the reviewer's spec: F2 said "the same rule as
    `survival()`".
  - **Fix:** P(reached ≥ i), where `reached` is the highest passed
    ticket, as the rollup defines it (`src/campaign.rs:2375-2378`).
  - **Test:** the curve never increases, and it matches values computed
    by hand on a fixture with a skipped ticket.
- **R44-3: the public board links into the console.** `render_board`
  writes `<a href="/">🚀 Benchmark Runner &amp; Console</a>` (`:742`).
  - On the VPS that link is dead, and the public board must not link
    into the console (`gui-spec.md` §4.1).
  - **Fix:** remove it.
- **R44-4: a career containing a dropped phase is still published.**
  - `load_raw` drops hidden, poisoned and harness-fault cells, and the
    comment at `:164` says "the career check below also drops a chain
    that contains one". No such check exists in `careers()` (`:423`).
  - With phase 2 poisoned, phase 3 still appears, under "unfinished",
    with career totals that include phase 2.
  - **Fix:** don't publish a career with a dropped cell, as F1's "the
    other filters stay" asks.
  - **Test:** a career with a poisoned middle phase doesn't appear.
- **R44-5: leftovers.**
  - The old renderer stays in `src/report/portal.rs` under six
    `#[allow(dead_code)]` attributes (`SqlBoardCell`, `render_sql_board`,
    the ladder helpers). Delete it.
  - `portal` still prints `Deploy: rsync -az --delete {}/
    <vps>:/srv/bench/` (`src/main.rs:1663`), which skips every
    `publish.sh` check. Print `scripts/publish.sh` instead.

**Next** (for a later round):
